Meridian Support

Support page last updated: 2026-06-17

Use this page for Meridian app support, privacy questions, account deletion help, data export help, bug reports, or TestFlight feedback.

Contact support

  • Email: lindberg.erick720@gmail.com
  • GitHub issues: github.com/ericklindberg/life-manager/issues

What to include

  • Your device type and OS version.
  • The Meridian app version or TestFlight build number if available.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • Whether the issue affects capture, tasks, projects, notes, reminders, sync, sharing, widgets, Apple Watch, export, or account deletion.

Privacy and account requests

For privacy questions, data export requests, shared-board access questions, or account deletion help, include “Privacy” or “Account deletion” in the email subject so the request can be handled directly.

Privacy policy

Read Meridian’s privacy policy here: https://ericklindberg.github.io/wearcast-support/meridian/privacy.html.
