Frequently Asked Questions

How does WearCast work?

WearCast uses your current location to fetch weather data and provides AI-powered outfit recommendations based on the forecast.

Is my location data stored?

No. Your location is processed locally on your device and is not stored on our servers.

How do I change my notification settings?

You can change notification preferences within the WearCast app settings, or through your device's notification settings.

What weather data does WearCast use?

WearCast uses Open-Meteo for weather data, a free and open-source weather API.
